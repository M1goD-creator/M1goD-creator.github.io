Refund policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

If you cancel before I start

You get the full 40% prepayment back.

If you cancel after I’ve started

I keep the 40% prepayment, because it pays for the time I set aside for your project and the work already done. You don’t pay the remaining 60%. If you’re a private person in the EU, the rule below applies instead.

If the result doesn’t match what we agreed

You pay the remaining 60% only when the work is finished. If something doesn’t match our written agreement, I fix it for free before you pay.

Monthly support and hosting

You can cancel at any time. The service runs until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and I don’t charge you again. I don’t refund the rest of a month you’ve already started.

Consumers in the EU

If you order as a private person rather than a business, you may have a 14-day right to cancel. If you ask me to start within those 14 days, you pay only for the work done up to the day you cancel.

How to ask for a refund

Message me on Telegram or email migdev.business@gmail.com. I refund to the card you paid with within 14 days.
