Cookie policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This site uses no cookies

The site doesn’t set cookies, run analytics, use tracking pixels or load scripts and fonts from other companies. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner.

Links to other services

When you follow a link to Telegram, Instagram or your email app, you leave this site. Those services use their own cookies under their own policies.
