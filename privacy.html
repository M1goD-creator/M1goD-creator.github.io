Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Mig is run by Georgii Rezhepp, based in Belgrade, Serbia. I decide how your personal data is used. You can reach me at migdev.business@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no contact forms, no analytics and no cookies, and it doesn’t load third-party scripts or fonts. The order builder runs only in your browser and doesn’t save or send your choices. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. When you open the site, GitHub records your IP address to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. I don’t see or use these logs.

What I get when you contact me

When you write to me on Telegram, Instagram or by email, I receive what you send: your name or username, your contact details and information about your project. I use it to answer you, prepare a quote and do the work you order. Telegram, Instagram and Gmail handle these messages under their own privacy policies.

If you become a client, I also keep your name, business details and invoices, because Serbian accounting law requires it.

Why I’m allowed to use it

I use your data to prepare and carry out our agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and to meet my accounting and tax obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Serbia’s Law on Personal Data Protection gives you the same protections.

Who else sees it

I don’t sell your data or use it for advertising. I share it only when the work needs it: with my bank, Raiffeisen Bank, when you pay by card, and with my accountant for invoices.

How long I keep it

I keep messages about a project for 2 years after we last talk, unless you ask me to delete them sooner. I keep invoices for as long as Serbian law requires, currently 10 years.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of your data, ask me to correct it, delete it or stop using it, or ask for it in a format you can take elsewhere. You can also complain to Serbia’s Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Poverenik) or to the data protection authority in your EU country.

How to delete your data

Email migdev.business@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and tell me how you contacted me. I delete everything I’m not legally required to keep and confirm within 30 days.

Children

My services are for businesses and adults aged 18 or over. I don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has sent me their data, email me and I’ll delete it.

Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update the date at the top of this page.
